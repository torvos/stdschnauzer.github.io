Lia’s summer litter

Lia is expecting a litter this summer.

Here is the first picture of Lia’s puppies!  

Her puppies will be ready for their new homes mid September.

If you are interested in this litter write to us.

You can visit Lia’s page on this site and find her health record .

The sire of this litter will be AMCH Scarlights Seasar TD NA. ( click on Seasar’s name) . Seasar is an incredible dog who was born in Sweden and enjoyed a fabulous career in the US . He is a multiple all- breed Best in Show winner, the 2004 SSCA National Specialty winner  and  Best of Breed at the Westminster Kennel Club Show from 2003 to 2006.  Beyond his  extraordinary show carreer, Seasar is a dog of exceptional temperament which makes him a perfect match for Lia .
