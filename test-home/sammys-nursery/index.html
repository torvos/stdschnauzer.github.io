June 6th,

Lots has happened this week.  This is the famous people litter, we have a name for everyone, now we have to decide who will be who !! In no particular order

” Kiri” ( STC!) Champagne Oceans Eugenie Clark ( a famous oceans scientist who first use diving in research – like her mom and dad)

” Jasper” Champagne Rocky Colin Robson ( for Mount Robson the highest peak in the Canadian Rockies and Colin Robertson after which the mountain is named)

“Bertram” Champagne Bertie Wooster ( P.G. Wodehouse most famous character )

“Ginger”  Champagne Ginger Rogers  ( the famous actress and dancer)

The puppies are now officially little schnauzers, they have a routine which involves mainly requesting lots of petting in the morning – well all the time really, followed by a meal, a run around the big room – well several rounds- and collapse into a well earned coma.  Then we start again ! To Line and Michel’s delight, we have also trained ourselves to go to the litter box and sleep in our crates at night ( or the tunnel in the daytime, or in a pile)

We cannot approach the counter where the food is without being reminded that they are starving, even if they have just been fed ! Now if the big dogs get a cookie in their  crates, these little guys also need a treat !   We had fun this week running outside and playing with our ball sand box, playing in the steps and learning to go up and down, running up and down the hill, eating grass and generally spending our energy until our batteries ran out.  Today we got our first grooming too!  Enjoy the pictures from this week !

Monday, April 27

Here we are , together facing in the same direction ! For just a moment: from left to right:

Ms White Mr Green, Mr Blue an Ms Pink

We are growing at light speed and our eyes are just about to open, but none would do it for the camera just now.  They are a very sweet bunch of puppies, so far very well behaved !!

Ms Pink  is slightly smaller than her siblings, but not by much; these are substantial puppies.

Ms White is the chart topper, and can be distinguished by her lighter colouring.

 

 

 

Ms Green is the leader weight wise and has slightly lighter socks that the others , while Mr Blue is content to be in the middle of the pack.

You can tell by now that the boys have a stronger heads than the girls, and they are all very sweet.

 

 

 

Mr Blue and and Ms White having a rumble ! It is funny to see them role over as  means of getting in the direction they want to travel.

They are quite mobile, although still crawling and they are starting to practice push ups, a precursor to getting on all fours.

Mr Green and Ms  Pink having a cuddle snooze

 

 

 

 

And Sammy enjoying a visit from her Mom and Dad, showing off her puppies.

 

 

This coming week will be a big week in terms of development: the eyes will open, their hearing will kick in and…. they might get VERY mobile by the end of the week.  So far there is no question as to where they are: somewhere in their whelping box, next week we might have to start putting doors in.  The fun will begin !

See you next week!
