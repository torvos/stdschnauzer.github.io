CH Dinsdale Champagne N Diamonds “Ciara”

 

 CH Dinsdale Champagne N Diamonds “Ciara”

Ciara is our little Irish girl.Born on March 17, 2009 to our GCH Dinsdale Champagne Celebration CGN  “Lia” and AMCH Geistvoll Double Diamond “Dylan”

Ciara 12 weeks

 Ciara was bred to   Topher,  AM CH Lookin At Da Man and produced a wonderful litter of ten ! Check out her page!    son  CH Champagne Rulander Sweet Louie   “Louie” is residing with us .

 

 

Ciara is funny and affectionnate, has tons of energy and a super harsh coat reminiscent of the true working schnauzers. Ciara is also a super mom and produces nice puppies !
