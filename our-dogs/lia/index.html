GCHEX Dinsdale Champagne Celebration RN, CGN ‘Lia’

Lia

GCHEX Dinsdale Champagne Celebration RN, CGN

Lia winning Best Veteran in Specialty at 13 years.

Lia combines all of the desirable schnauzer attributes; she is feminine and robust at the same time; she has an incredibly sweet temperament , and is affectionate and  playful.  She has a fabulous harsh coat and seems to walk on air.  Lia started her show career when she was 6 months of age, earned her championship from the puppy classes and never looked back.  Along the way she earned  hundred group placements, an all-breed Best in Show and her Rally Novice  title making her a Grand Champion Excellent .  Lia was recognized as the top Schnauzer in Canada  in 2006 and 2007 .

Following her Rally Novice title, Lia was awarded “Grand Champion Excellent” certificate  ! This is the highest honors that can be received, to gain this title Lia had to receive 100+ group placements, a Best In Show award and a performance title.  all done with a smile 😉

Lia and Line at the Gala

August 18, 2012 Lia earns the title of Rally Novice !

Lia winning Best in Show

 

Lia has produced two wonderful litters one in 2009  from AM CH Geistvoll Double Diamond  “Dylan”  and her daughter Ciara resides with us. And one in 2011 from AMCH Scarlights Seasar ad her daughter came back to give us two wonderful litters.
