Ch Dinsdale’s Captain Tempête CGN

This is little Tempête the day he arrived home from Ontario!  He was 8weeks old !

Tempete was our first show dog and although he did not really enjoy it he finished his Canadian championship and even garnered a Group 2nd which gave him the honour of being number three schnauzer in Canada that year !

Of course he liked the treats that comes with the job!

Above all Tempête loves to play, here he is running with Breeze and Lia, enjoying the snow!

Of all our dogs, he his the one retriever ! and he does it with gusto!Above all Tempête loves to go for his walks, greet our visitors and maintain law and order in the yard!

 

Our Tempête is at the rainbow bridge :(.
