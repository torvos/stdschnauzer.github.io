GCH Champagne Ginger Rogers

Ginger is the little girl we kept from the breeding of our own Champagne Totally Bewitched ” Sammy” and Int CH Ave Concorde Magnifique ” Sammy” .  Ginger started her show career with a bang winning Best Baby Puppy in Show at her first appearance.  I had entered her in one show for practice !

Ginger went on to earn her Canadian championship all with group placements except for her first point, and went on to earn her Grand Championship ( all at breed level) shortly after, garnering 3 of 6 possible select awards the week end of the National Specialty!

Ginger is affectionate, outgoing  and full of mischief.  She particularly like to torture her partner in crime Louie.  Ginger is very much the princess, she loves hiking, and long walks.

We are looking forward to her becoming a mom soon !
