Let us see !

Posted on February 20, 2013

There is always excitement in the house when the new puppies arrive.  The older dogs all want to have a glimpse of the newcomers !  Mom Ciara on the other hand is not too keen on visitors at this point; so everyone has to make due with looking through the gate in the hope of seeing the new pups.  Here is Tempête, Lia and Louie trying their luck!

Ciara is funny, she blinks when you take her picture,  and seem to say, take your picture and go away !  The little ones are so wiggly it is difficult to catch a good angle !
