Flute wins an Award of Merit !

Posted on May 16, 2013

Last week end the Standard Schnauzer Club of America held its National Specialty at the Purina Farms in Grey Summit, Missouri.  This was one big schnauzer show week end  called the Schnauzapaloza as there also were specialties for the Miniature and Giant Schnauzers.  There were schnauzers everywhere !

The entry for the Standard schnauzer was the largest ever, in conformation only there were 200 competing ! There were schnauzers in herding, sheeps and ducks, obedience, rally and agility as well !

This makes it even more special that Flute, Breeze and Bennie’s daughter, shown by her owner, Brooke Thomas from Wisconsin won an Award of Merit at this very special event!

Here is Brooke and Flute showing their style and below, the coveted ribbon !

 

Thanks Brooke for showing Flute so well !

Congratulations to both of you!
