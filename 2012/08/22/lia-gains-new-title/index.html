Lia gains new title !

Posted on August 22, 2012

Since the beginning of the summer Lia and Line have been training and practicing in Rally Obedience. Lia trialled at the Bathurst show in July and again at the Kennebecasis Obedience Club Show on August 18th and 19th.

Lia finished her title with three scores of  91, 92 and 96 (/100) making her

GCH Dinsdale Campagne Celebration RN, CGN !!

Here she is posing with Line and judge Michael Calhoun after completing her third leg.

 

This was also Bruno first outing without his mom and going camping with the girls, Sofie and Lia .  Here he is relaxing after a good play!

They had so much fun during the week end at the show that upon return Bruno and Sofie slept and slept ….zzzzzz.

In July we also celebrated Sammy, Oakley and Finnegan’s first birthday. Happy Birthday guys !!

Here is Sammy sporting her party hat ! She had a lot of fun at her party, once the hat got lost !
